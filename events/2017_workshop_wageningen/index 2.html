2017: 8th Dutch Development Economics Network Workshop

The eighth workshop, hosted by Wageningen University.
Workshop
Event date

May 11, 2017

Wageningen University
Development Economics Group

When
11–12 May 2017

Where
Leeuwenborch building, Hollandseweg 1, 6706 KN Wageningen

Download the programme

2 days 10 papers 3 early-research talks

The eighth edition returned to Wageningen’s Leeuwenborch building with ten papers over two days. Topics ran from the success and failure of new stock markets and cooperation in polygynous households to entrepreneurship edutainment in Egypt, sub-national inequality, financial development and industrial pollution, information on polls in South Africa, savings groups for people with disabilities in Uganda, payments for ecosystem services and financial diaries.

A session of early results from the DFID research programme covered imported inputs and product innovation, mobile money for small businesses in Kenya and the aspirations of small retailers in Jakarta.

The workshop was co-sponsored by Tilburg University (Department of Economics), Wageningen University (Development Economics Group), the University of Lucerne and the DFID research programme on innovation and growth in low-income countries.
